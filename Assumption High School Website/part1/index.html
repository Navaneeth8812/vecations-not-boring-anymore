ASSUMPTION HIGH SCHOOL SULTHAN BATHERY

About

Assumption High School was established in June 1982 as a wake-up call for the lifting of the historic Bathery. The foundation of this school is the long-sightedness and eminent leadership of honourable Joseph Cuttackilachan. The school, which was started exclusively for girls, has been providing facilities for boys since June 2000. The institution will stand for the cultural equilibrium of Wayanad by protecting minority rights and with an emphasis on value-based education.

Facilities

Assumption High School is at the forefront of Wayanad Education District in terms of education and extracurricular activities. The school, which has a pass percentage of 97 per cent in the first year, maintains a high success rate in later years. 18 Classrooms, Science Lab, Computer Lab, Atal Thinking Lab, Library Mathematics Library, Projector, Laptop, Speaker, etc. are set up in all classrooms without any set-up. There is a spacious playground available for children to play and their physical development, lunch for students of Class VIII and adequate water.
