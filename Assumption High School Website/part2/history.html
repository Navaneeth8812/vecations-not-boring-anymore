Our History

Our heritage

Assumption High School, Sulthan Bathery, was established in June 1982 as a beacon of educational excellence during a period of remarkable growth in the historic town of Bathery. The institution was founded through the visionary leadership and farsighted efforts of the late Rev. Fr. Joseph Vettikuzhichalil, whose commitment to quality education laid the foundation for generations of learners. The school initially began as an institution exclusively for girls and later expanded its doors to boys in June 2000, responding to the aspirations and educational needs of the local community

Story of Land

The story of Assumption High School is deeply intertwined with the rich cultural heritage of Wayanad. This land, celebrated for its breathtaking landscapes and historic significance, is home to legendary figures such as Pazhassi Raja, Edachena Kunkan, and Karinthandan. The majestic peaks of Chembra, Banasura, and Ambukuthi Hills stand as silent witnesses to centuries of history. Wayanad's cultural richness is reflected in treasures such as the Edakkal Caves, the ancient Jain temples of Bathery and Punjavayal, the sacred Thirunelli Temple, and numerous megalithic monuments. These landmarks testify to a civilization that has flourished through the ages and continue to inspire future generations.

The Birth Of Assumption High School

As the educational aspirations of the growing community increased, the need for a high school became evident. In response, Assumption High School was established in June 1982. The institution emerged as a symbol of progress and hope, providing quality secondary education to students of Bathery and the surrounding areas. From its humble beginnings, the school steadily grew in strength, infrastructure, and academic excellence. The values envisioned by its founders continue to guide the institution even today.

Vision And Values

The true strength of Assumption High School lies not merely in its buildings or achievements, but in its commitment to nurturing responsible citizens. The institution strives to cultivate self-knowledge, self-discipline, compassion, leadership, and a spirit of service among its students. Guided by the ideals of truth, goodness, and excellence, the school continues to empower young minds to become agents of positive change in society.

Continuing Legacy

Having completed more than four decades of dedicated service to education, Assumption High School proudly carries forward a legacy built on faith, knowledge, and community spirit. With gratitude for its rich past and confidence in its future, the institution continues to illuminate lives through education, preparing students to meet the challenges of an ever-changing world while remaining rooted in enduring values
