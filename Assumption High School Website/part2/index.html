ASSUMPTION HIGH SCHOOL SULTHAN BATHERY

About School

Established in 1982, Assumption High School has been a beacon of quality education in Sulthan Bathery, Wayanad. For over four decades, the school has remained committed to nurturing young minds through academic excellence, moral values, and holistic development. Our institution strives to create an environment where students are encouraged to think critically, act responsibly, and grow into confident individuals prepared to meet the challenges of an ever-changing world.

Our Vision

To inspire and empower every student to become a lifelong learner, responsible citizen, and compassionate leader who contributes positively to society.


Our Mission

To empower students for future success through academic excellence, innovation, and critical thinking, while nurturing moral values, social responsibility, and a vibrant participation in sports and the arts.

Our Values

Excellence

We encourage every student to strive for their personal best in academics, sports, and life.

Integrity

We promote honesty, responsibility, and ethical behavior in all aspects of school life.

Respect

We foster a culture of mutual respect, inclusivity, and understanding.

Innovation

We embrace modern teaching methods and technology to enhance learning.

Compassion

We encourage empathy, kindness, and service to the community.

Academic Excellence

At Assumption High School, we believe that education extends beyond textbooks. Our dedicated faculty members work closely with students to ensure a strong academic foundation while encouraging curiosity, creativity, and independent thinking. Through modern teaching practices, well-equipped laboratories, library resources, and technology-enabled learning environments, we strive to make education meaningful and engaging.
